How to play

Six pieces of lore from one game, revealed one at a time. Name the game.

  • You get six guesses. Every miss reveals the next piece of lore, and they get more direct as you go.
  • Misses tell you whether the answer is earlier or later than your guess, and whether the genre or developer match.
  • Pick titles from the list; abbreviations like BotW or RDR2 work. Stuck? Skipping costs a guess and reveals the next clue.
  • A new game arrives at midnight. The heart is your daily streak; every round you win in a row builds your round streak.
  • Missed a day? The calendar holds every past daily. Free play has filters and a hard mode.
